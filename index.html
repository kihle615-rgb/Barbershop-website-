Guard 0 · the premise

No line where it begins.

That is the whole job. Skin becomes shadow becomes hair, and nowhere in between can you point at the step. Anyone can take length off. The work is in the part you are not supposed to notice.

What you are supposed to notice is the edge — the line-up across the forehead and down the temples, cut straight enough to argue with. Soft where it fades, hard where it lands. Get both right and the cut holds its shape for a fortnight.

Precision. Confidence. Character.
